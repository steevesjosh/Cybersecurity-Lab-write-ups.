Course Completion: Phishing Email Analysis
**🛡️ Overview**
This repository documents my progress and completion of the "Phishing Email Analysis" course on LetsDefend. This course focused on developing the practical skills required by a Security Operations Center (SOC) analyst to identify, inspect, and mitigate social engineering threats delivered via email.
**🔍 Key Learning Objectives & Topics Covered**
Email Header Analysis: Inspecting routing information, sender authenticity, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records to spot spoofed domains.

Payload Investigation: Safely handling and analyzing malicious attachments and embedded links without risking internal network security.

Triage & Response: Determining whether a reported email is benign, spam, or a targeted phishing campaign and taking appropriate remediation steps.

**🚀 Why This Matters**
Phishing remains one of the most common initial access vectors for attackers. Mastering email analysis enables a SOC analyst to quickly dissect suspicious communications, protect organizational assets, and prevent credential harvesting or malware deployment.
