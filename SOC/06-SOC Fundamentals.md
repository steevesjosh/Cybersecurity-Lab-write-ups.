Course Completion: SOC Fundamentals
**🛡️ Overview**
This repository documents my progress and completion of the "SOC Fundamentals" course on LetsDefend. This course introduced the foundational concepts, architecture, roles, and daily operations of a Security Operations Center (SOC).

**🔍 Key Learning Objectives & Topics Covered**
SOC Introduction & Architecture: Understanding what a Security Operations Center is, how it fits into overall enterprise security, and the different tiers of SOC analysts.

Core Technologies: Gaining familiarity with essential tools like SIEM (Security Information and Event Management), EDR (Endpoint Detection and Response), and ticketing systems.

Incident Response Lifecycle: Learning the standard steps of handling security alerts—from initial triage and investigation to containment and remediation.

**🚀 Why This Matters**
SOC Fundamentals lay the groundwork for any aspiring cybersecurity professional. Understanding how security alerts are monitored, triaged, and escalated is essential for protecting organizational infrastructure against real-world cyber threats.
