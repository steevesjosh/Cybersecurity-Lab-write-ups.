Course Completion: Cyber Kill Chain
**🛡️ Overview**
This repository documents my progress and completion of the "Cyber Kill Chain" course on LetsDefend. This course focused on Lockheed Martin's Cyber Kill Chain framework, breaking down the sequential phases an adversary takes during a cyber attack from initial reconnaissance to objective completion.


**🔍 Key Learning Objectives & Topics Covered**
Phases of an Attack: Understanding the core stages of the Cyber Kill Chain:

Reconnaissance

Weaponization

Delivery

Exploitation

Installation

Command & Control (C2)

Actions on Objectives

Defensive Interruption: Learning how security controls and SOC analysts can disrupt an attacker's progress at various points along the chain.

Incident Triage Application: Applying the kill chain model to real-world attack scenarios to map out adversary movements and isolate compromised systems.
**
🚀 Why This Matters**
The Cyber Kill Chain framework helps security professionals conceptualize how attacks unfold. By understanding each phase, a SOC analyst can identify early indicators of compromise (IoCs), break the attack lifecycle, and prevent full-scale security breaches.
